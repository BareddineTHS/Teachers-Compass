    Short guides

    How-To

    Four quick guides to get the most out of the materials.

    Make it your own

    Every file is editable. Change the names, the examples, the timings and the level so it fits your learners.

    1. Open the file in Google Drive.
    2. Click File, then Make a copy. The copy goes to your own Drive.
    3. Edit your copy freely. The original stays untouched for everyone else.
    4. To use it in Word, click File, Download, then Microsoft Word (.docx).

    How the hyperlinks work

    Many documents have a clickable table of contents and links between related materials.

    1. Click a title in the contents page to jump to that lesson.
    2. Blue underlined text opens a related file, for example the worksheet that goes with a lesson plan.
    3. In a downloaded PDF, links work in most PDF readers. Hold Ctrl while clicking in Word.
    4. To come back, use your reader's Back button or Alt + Left arrow.

    Navigating the Drive

    Folders follow the same order everywhere: level, then textbook, then unit, then type of material.

    2BACTicket to English 2Unit 3Worksheets
    1. Use the search bar at the top of Drive and type a unit or a grammar point.
    2. Switch to List view to see file names in full.
    3. Click the star on a folder you use often to find it later under Starred.

    Isolating files from compilations

    Some files are compilations of many lessons. To keep only the pages you need, print them to a new PDF.

    1. Open the PDF and press Ctrl + P (or Cmd + P on a Mac).
    2. As the printer, choose Save as PDF or Microsoft Print to PDF.
    3. Under Pages, choose Custom and type the range, for example 12-15.
    4. Click Save. You now have a separate file with just those pages.
